About Our Service

Dedicated Doorstep Physiotherapy in Jaipur

Rahul Physio – Home Visit Physiotherapy provides convenient physiotherapy services at your doorstep in Jaipur. Home visits are available for musculoskeletal conditions, pain management, mobility and exercise-based rehabilitation. Prior appointment is recommended.

Home-Based Physiotherapy

No stressful hospital queues or difficult travel. Receive focused, personalized care in the safe and comfortable environment of your home.

Personalized Treatment Plans

Each session is designed around your specific condition, baseline mobility, functional goals, and physician guidelines for progressive recovery.

Exercise-Based Rehabilitation

Focusing on strengthening weakened muscle groups, correcting kinetic imbalances, and restoring natural biomechanics with active drills.

Mobility & Functional Rehabilitation

Targeting bed mobility, sit-to-stand transfers, walking stride, and balance confidence for seniors and post-illness individuals.

Orthopedic & Sports Rehabilitation

Structured recovery for back pain, cervical strain, joint stiffness, ACL/ligament sprains, tendon issues, and post-fracture recovery.

Neurological Rehabilitation

Facilitating motor relearning, gait retraining, tone management, and functional independence for stroke, hemiplegia, and Parkinson’s.
